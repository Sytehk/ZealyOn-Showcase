# ZealyOn

**Strategy discovery, market insights, and trading tools**

ZealyOn is a cross-platform product for people who want to explore trading strategies, review their performance, and follow strategy activity. It brings research and market context together with account views, alerts, and live strategy tools.

## What the product covers

- **Discover and compare strategies:** review performance measures and historical results to understand how a strategy has behaved.
- **Follow strategy activity:** receive entry and exit alerts and review a shared alert feed.
- **Track accounts and positions:** see balances, open positions, and trade history across supported account environments.
- **Explore markets:** browse market information and charts alongside strategy details.
- **Use across devices:** Flutter applications and web experiences support the product on mobile, desktop, and browser.

## Engineering scope

The product combines a Flutter client with Rust services and data integrations. Supabase and PostgreSQL support application data and account workflows. Strategy analysis, market data, notifications, and external provider connections are handled as separate parts of the system.

## Technology

Flutter and Dart, Rust, Supabase, PostgreSQL, market-data and account-provider integrations.
